All Is Lost
July 26, 2026
Defeat
All... is... lost... A sentiment rarely uttered but commonly felt.

As I go through the world, I see it constantly in the eyes of people from all walks of life.

One would assume the feeling is acute terror, but the human brain is an incredible machine; the feeling of doom has a very short time-constant, and it transitions from doom to dread to apathy to mania.

From my experience failure never arises from the acute terror, it is triggered from apathy. The sound of defeat of a man is not a gasp but a whimper.
Purpose
The positive side of this is, you never lose if you never give up; however this also means to lose one must give up.

And, frankly there's nothing more depressing then seeing a person who has given up. Many sad souls spend their whole lives quitting; never perservering; surrending due to pain and apathy.

If I've learned one thing in life, the quitters never end up happy. From the poor irish farmer to the silicon valley billionaire; from the high-school dropout to the Quantum Physics Ph.D; I've met them all and the one through line I can draw is that the "successful" quitter is chronically depressed and the perservant man is content.

The 0.1% quitter may get lucky, but they will never be satisfied, they will always yearn for something better in life, that they will never find; because purpose is instrinsic, you cannot develop that purpose if you cannot perservere.

Purpose is crafted on the last stretch rep, the painful last push, the stressful all-nighter, the tough big leap; this is obvious to the perserverant but lost on the weak as like an addict they are strapped to the hedonic treadmill, yearning for the comfort of mediocrity.
All is Lost Syndrome
The prior content is necessary background knowledge, the thing I want to talk today about is "All is Lost" syndrome.

Jonestown
In 1978, the U.S senator Leo Ryan went to visit the Peoples Temple in Jonestown, Guyana. The senator was on a mission to determine if the people of this settlement were being held hostage, there was significant critical media coverage of the settlement which caused the leaders of the Peoples temple to plan the visit meticulously.

The situation was particularly tense as the senator had brought numerous family members of the residents of Jonestown, many who believed the Temple had kidnapped their relatives.

However, by the end of the trip after interviewing many members of the settlement the senator was shockingly impressed, he conveyed to the leaders of Jonestown that he thought the place was beautiful and though some wanted to leave, that his report back to the U.S would be broadly positive. One would consider this a huge success that a member of the U.S federal government would give his approval to a cult in Guyana. The leaders of the Peoples Temple were excited that it had gone so well.

That night, the penultimate leader Jim Jones proclaimed "I have failed... all is lost", he ordered the assassination of the senator and the murder-suicide of all 900 residents of Jonestown including 300 children.

Jones had so vigorously expected and predicted defeat, the concept of success had become an impossibility. The terror of the visit and the chaos of the town had numbed all hope to apathy, and in that apathy the success is meaningless and in fact must be defeated.
Deterministic Defeat
When one gets in the headspace of quitting and apathy, defeat is guaranteed.
The 2 outcomes become:

Failure. Upon failure the brain's world model has been satisfied. The feeling of apathy becomes justified to the self and any effort feels pointless. This creates a cyclic process where a person quits sooner, fails faster and eventually loses their sense of self.

Success. Upon success, one could argue this is the most dangerous outcome. When a person somehow succeeds or survives despite the mental act of quitting and the sense of apathy, rather than being fulfilled by victory a very odd thing happens. The brain's world model has been violated, defeat has so readily been predicted that they act to destroy their own success.

A form of self-destructive mania is invoked. Rash, unjustifiable decisions are made because they just want it all to be over, they've mentally prepared themselves to move on and remaining in the situation causes an acute stress.

This behaviour seems insane, but it is quite common. When you look at the failure of startups, you'll often see at the end there are manic, very strange decisions made not many go quiet into the night. When you look at the end of relationships, it's rarely a quiet goodbye from drifting apart and often an emotional explosion.
Lesson
My interpretation of my life events has been to never give up. When you quit once, you quit twice and you can get trapped into being a chronic quitter and it leads to a very unsatisfying life.

Silicon Valley today seems to attract the striver, it's the frontier, where opportunistic men of any background can make it. I think this is a great thing, it casts a wide net, but I think a side effect of this is it can accidentally reward the lucky quitter.

Many of the talking heads online fall into this category and expound their wisdom and advice when they are deeply troubled personally. Losers encouraging the youth to follow their path of apathy and quitting.
